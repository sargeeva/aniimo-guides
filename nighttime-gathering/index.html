How platform decorating scores

Your Personal Contribution is the Contribution from items you turn in at your current Park plus the Contribution from your platform decorations. Each piece of furniture on the platform turns its Habitability into Contribution.

What this means: to reach 70,000 with 100 items or fewer, your items need to average 700+ Contribution each. Wonder Night pieces give up to 1,974, so about 38 of them hit the cap. Regular furniture tops out around 1,250, so a regular-only setup takes about 84 items.
Keep your platform Contribution when you move. Contribution from turn-ins is cleared if you leave your Park, but platform Contribution and your overall personal total stay with you.

Best setups

Each of these reaches the 70,000 cap within the 100-item and 80,000-load limits. Tap "Load into planner" to tweak one.

Jelly Bean value: every Wonder Night item gives about 12 Contribution per Weird Jelly Bean, from Sugar Puff (1,974 for 165) down to the small candies (183 for 15). The bean cost of hitting 70,000 is about the same whichever items you pick, so buy the biggest ones to stay under 100 items. Regular Wishstar furniture costs exactly its Habitability in Wishstars, so it's always 1 Wishstar per Contribution.

Turn-in quests

The other source of Contribution: offerings for the Pumpkin Aeon at the center of the Park.

  • You can hold 1 turn-in quest at a time. Each is worth 1,000–8,000 Contribution, depending on its quality.
  • Quests ask for event dishes and crops (Magic Pumpkin, Memory Lanternfruit dishes), Home materials (Rock, Clay, Shell, Ore…), or Home Coin / Wishstars.
  • You start with 5 acceptances, gain +3 daily at 04:00, and can bank up to 10. An unfinished quest auto-refreshes at 04:00 and still uses up its acceptance.
  • If you haven't accepted a quest, you can refresh the offers with Home Coin: 10,000, then 20,000, 30,000 … up to 100,000. They also refresh on a timer.
  • Turn-in Contribution is cleared if you move Parks. A Private RV Park with stable neighbours is safer.
