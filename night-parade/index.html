How the event works

Night Parade has two activities. Both feed a sticker book called the Night Journal, and filling the Journal unlocks the big rewards.

Night Candy House

Do your daily Parade quests to earn up to 3 Night Candies a day. Spend them to decorate the Candy House through 4 tiers (30 candies in total). Each tier gives a gift and a Journal sticker.

Night Perfume Making

5 Aniimo visitors arrive on days 1, 3, 5, 7 and 9. Blend each one a perfume from 3 ingredients that matches their taste. Each one you finish gives Sparkling Crystals and a Journal sticker.

Night Journal

There are 9 stickers to collect: 4 Candy House tiers plus 5 visitors. Collecting them unlocks 5 milestone rewards, and the last one is the Halloween Frolic avatar frame.

Night Candy House

Night Candies come only from the daily Parade quests. Unspent candies carry over with no cap, so missing a day just means catching up later.

Night Perfume Making

Each visitor offers 7 ingredients. You pick 3. Every ingredient pushes the perfume's three flavor scales (Sweet, Rich and Airy) up or down, and the totals have to meet the visitor's requirements. Some visitors also insist on one specific ingredient. You can only submit a perfume that passes.

Visitors come one at a time in a fixed order, and each stays until the event ends, so you can't miss one. The day count starts when the event opens for you.

Recipe tester

Try a blend before you commit it in game. Pick a visitor, then tap 3 of their ingredients. The green dashed box on each scale is the range the visitor accepts.

Night Journal

The Journal holds 9 stickers. You get one for each Candy House tier you finish and one for each visitor you make a perfume for. The in-game Journal shows how many stickers each milestone needs.
