Notes

  • Each Whisperwake amber gives 1 Lumin Amber - Whisperwake Isles, 2,000 Aniimo EXP and 200 Safaris. Feed the amber to a Whisperwake Branch to level it up.
  • Each area's Gold badge needs you to collect that area's Lumin Amber.
  • Times are in-game, not real time. The game splits the day into four periods: Dawn (06:00–08:00), Noon (08:00–17:00), Dusk (17:00–20:00) and Night (20:00–06:00).
  • The tide spots are named "night low tide" and "high tide" in the game data. Whisperwake's tide shifts as day turns to night, and the game pairs low tide with night, so check the high-tide amber during the day.
  • "Linked to a questline" means the amber sits inside quest content: the Sugarfish Princess story, the Puffby honey story, or the Prawnch "Three Trials of the Rapids". You may need to progress that quest before it's reachable.
