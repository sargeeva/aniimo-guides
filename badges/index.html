How badge tiers work

Most area badges come in four tiers. Each tier needs more of the same area, and each one adds a privilege that helps you in that area.

Notes

  • The game sends each condition's wording and your progress from the server. The client only lists condition IDs and targets, so some steps here are worked out from those targets and the area settings. Each one is labeled.
  • The area catch milestones (40% / 70% / 100%) come from each area's settings. The game text confirms that 40% gives the Bronze badge.
  • Badges marked Coming later exist in the data but are flagged as not obtainable yet, mostly future Whisperwake Isles areas.
