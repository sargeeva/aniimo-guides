Aniimo Outings

Send Aniimo from your RV Park on trips for specialties, Held Items and a chance at a Mysterious Aniimo Egg that can inherit their form and mutation, including Prismana, Sparkling and Alpha.

Quick facts

Open Aniimo Outings from the Park Board at your RV Park, pick Aniimo staying at the Park, pick a trip length, and collect rewards when they return.

Two rules to know: Aniimo staying at the Park can't join combat or exploration parties. And recalling a trip early loses all of its rewards.

The Mysterious Aniimo Egg

This is why most players run Outings. An Aniimo that returns from a trip can bring back an egg tied to itself.

  • 1
    Game text: at most 1 egg per trip. The in-game wording ("Aniimo bring back at most 1 Aniimo Egg per trip") doesn't say whether that's 1 per Aniimo or 1 for the whole outing, and a trip can send up to 3 Aniimo together. Not yet confirmed by player runs. The longer the trip, the higher the chance of an egg, so Camping (20 h) gives the best odds.
  • 2
    Same family. The egg's Aniimo family is related to the Aniimo that went on the trip.
  • 3
    Can inherit form and mutation. The egg has a chance to inherit the trip Aniimo's form and mutation, including the rare Prismana Form and mutations such as Sparkling and Alpha. Alpha counts as a mutation in the game's own terms, though the item text only names Prismana and Sparkling as examples.
  • 4
    Hatches in 5 hours (300 minutes) in an Outpost or Home Hatchinator. One speed-up item can be used per egg, and it can be petted to speed it up within the usual daily limits. For comparison, a Mini Egg takes 2.5 h and a Dreamy Egg 24 h.
  • 5
    Legendary-quality item (in-game id 4040120).
Farming tip: send your Prismana-form, Sparkling or Alpha Aniimo on Camping trips. Each one gives a chance at an egg that inherits the same form or mutation.

Aniimo that can't bring eggs back

About odds

The exact chances (egg per trip, form or mutation inheritance, Held Item quality) aren't in the game client. The server rolls them, and the client's reward tables for Outings point to server-only data. Everything on this page comes from the client's item text and settings. Treat any percentage you see elsewhere as community testing, not datamined numbers.
