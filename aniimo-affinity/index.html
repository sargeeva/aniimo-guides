The limits, explained

  • Shared pool: 60 per day, 420 per week (per Aniimo). Home time, Party time, Alphas, Pathfinder Challenges, Egg Heist and defeating Aniimo all add up toward this one cap. Home and Party also have their own smaller caps inside it (20 and 10 per day).
  • Petting is separate: 30 per pet, at most 30 per day (once a day) and 210 per week. It doesn't touch the shared pool.
  • Resonance has no cap. Each Resonance Star-Up gives +10 and each Resonance & Ascension gives +20, on top of everything else.
  • So the daily maximum is 90 (30 from petting + 60 from the shared pool), plus any Resonance bonuses. The weekly caps (210 + 420 = 630) are exactly 7 days of that, so playing every day never hits a weekly wall early.
What each level takes at the max rate (90/day): Lv. 2 on day 2, Lv. 3 on day 6, and Lv. 4 (max) on day 14. Resonance bonuses shave days off.
The game shows "…Affinity gained today/this week through [source] has reached the limit" when you hit a cap. If you only use some sources, you'll fill the pool more slowly. For example, Home and Party time alone give at most 30/day toward the 60 pool.

Fastest daily routine (per Aniimo)

  1. Pet it in your Home once: +30 (fills its petting cap for the day).
  2. Leave it in your Home about 67 minutes (+20) or keep it in your party about 33 minutes (+10). Doing both gives +30 toward the pool.
  3. Fill the rest of the 60 pool with combat: Alphas and Pathfinder Challenges give 10 each, Egg Heist 5, and wild Aniimo 1 each.
  4. Resonance bonuses (+10 / +20) are a free extra if you're upgrading that Aniimo anyway.
Several Aniimo at once: because limits are per Aniimo, everyone in your Home earns Home time at the same time, and everyone in your party earns Party time and combat Affinity. Rotating Aniimo through your party and Home levels many in parallel.
