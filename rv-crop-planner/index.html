RV Crop Planner

The best nonstop farm setup for every Home Store item you can make at home: how many plots, trees, wells and mines of each ingredient keep it running with nothing left waiting, and the net profit per hour it earns at your RV level.

12
Fill my farm: each item's setup is the biggest balanced one that fits the Farmland, Woodland, Wells, Mines and stations your RV level lets you place, with the whole farm on that one item. Every ingredient is grown or made on the spot, so nothing runs out and nothing piles up. Switch to Set a rate inside an item to size a smaller setup. Use Plots kept free and Trees kept free to keep Farmland and Woodland free for other things; every setup and profit figure then uses only what's left.
Profit per hour = items made per hour × Home Store base price. Net profit per hour is that minus the seeds you buy. Profit per plot (net profit per plot per hour) divides it by the Farmland plots and Woodland trees the setup uses, so you can see which item makes the most from each bit of farm space (wells, mines and stations aren't counted as plots). The store also picks a few items each day at ×3, ×5 or ×10, which isn't included. Byproducts (like the extra wood from trees) aren't counted either.
Energy per hour is for feeding your Aniimo: items made per hour × the energy each food gives (the same number the game shows under "Provides Energy"). Picking Energy in the sort menu shows only foods, best first. Bowl time sorts foods by how long all 3 full bowls of that food keep your Aniimo fed. Full bowl times use the game's numbers: your Home has 3 food bowls that hold 99 each (297 food), and every Aniimo eats 10 Energy per minute, so the bowls run out faster the more Aniimo you place (8 at RV 2 up to 47 at RV 22).
Timing: crops and trees use their grow time. Crafting stations, Wells, Mines and Aniimo beds run on workload, so they depend on your Aniimo's Home work level (Work Lv 1–4: 60, 75, 90 or 105 workload per minute). Crops with a temperature need are assumed to be in the right climate. Quick Formulas (Ecological Module, for crops and trees) and Resource Detector boosts (wells, sandcastle and Aniimo beds) are used automatically from the RV level that unlocks their module level, whenever they give more per hour; they show a "Quick" tag. Turn off the Quick Formulas button to plan without them. Powered mode isn't used.
Hybrid setups: tick the box to see the best mix of products to run at the same time at your RV level. They share your Farmland, Woodland, Wells, Aniimo beds and stations, and the mix is picked for the most net profit per hour (or energy per hour when sorting by Energy or Bowl time). It's worked out as a linear program and then rounded to whole plots, trees and wells. It's on by default and always considers everything your Home can make at your RV level (crafted goods, raw crops, and mine and well goods like Silver Ore), whatever the type filter shows. Products that need ingredients from outside your Home are left out.
Limited-time recipes: season and collab recipes show an end date and drop off this page by themselves once it passes, using the end time in the game data (the Halloween season and Captain Spud collab recipes end Dec 9, 2026 at 23:59 UTC). The Harvest Moon Festival recipes (Moondew Radish, Waxing Moon Pepper and the Umbral dishes) are already gone.
RV level is the highest requirement anywhere in the chain: the facility levels ("Reach RV Level X") and, for premium recipes, the RV module level that unlocks them. Placement limits per RV level come from the game data (e.g. Farmland 4 at RV 1 up to 42 at RV 22).
