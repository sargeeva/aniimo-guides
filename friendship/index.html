Aniimo Friendship Guide

How Friend Affinity works, the fastest ways to raise it, what each of the 6 levels unlocks, and how to become Illusory Friends and create an Illusory Aniimo.

Quick facts

Friendship in Aniimo is a per-friend number called Affinity (internally "friendship" / "intimacy"). It climbs through 6 named levels, each unlocking perks with that one friend.

The 6 Affinity levels

Ranges, perks and level-up messages come straight from friendship_level_data and friendship_level_func_data. The badge grows from a sprout into a tree as the friendship deepens.

Heads-up: a few in-game tooltip strings disagree with the level table. They place Perfect Sync at the level-3 badge and Face Cradle at the level-4 badge. The level table, which the Affinity panel actually reads, puts them at levels 4 and 5. The levels shown here follow the table. Snapshot invitations unlock at Affinity level 3, according to their own text string.

How to earn Affinity

From friendship_level_up_data. The game caps the total at 100 Affinity per friend per day (FRIENDSHIP_DAILY_LIMIT), and each activity also has its own daily cap. Capped activities add up to 160, so pick any mix that reaches 100.

Multiplayer dungeons that count: Duo Sanctums, Holo-Battle Interlink and Operation: Egg Heist. "Interaction Gestures" means using a two-person emote with the friend, and the gestures you can use depend on your Affinity level.

Fastest daily routine (100/day)

Option A: no combat

Visit Home (20) + 2 chats (20) + 1 emote (20) + 2 Alpha/Omega kills (40) = 100

Option B: co-op focused

1 multiplayer dungeon (40) + 3 Alpha/Omega kills (60) = 100. It also gives the most Illusory-Friend rolls at the highest odds

Affinity Storage & Companion Memories

The Affinity info panel also has an Affinity Storage page. The in-game text says you collect Companion Memories each day, up to 3 points per day, from three sources: playing a multiplayer match with the friend, gifting them an Aniimo in Companion Mode, and logging in. Stored points auto-convert to Affinity at 1 point per day, and you can also convert them by hand ("Convert {0} into Affinity").

Illusory: Illusory Friends & Illusory Aniimo

Aniimo Illusory

In the game code Illusory is called "Variant" (petVariantInteract, PET_LABEL_MASK.VARIANT) and your Illusory Friend is your "Special Friend". The in-game description:

"Daily interactions have a chance of making you Illusory Friends. Once you become Illusory Friends, you can pair your Aniimo with theirs to cuddle and become Illusory Aniimo."

How to get an Illusory Aniimo

  1. Reach Soulmate (Affinity level 6)

    Aniimo Illusory is one of the Soulmate unlocks in friendship_level_data, and the system config agrees (CHANGE_FRIEND_FRIENDSHIPLV = 6). Below 4,500 Affinity, interactions don't roll for Illusory Friendship at all, so max the friendship first.

  2. Keep interacting daily to trigger the Illusory-Friend roll

    Once you're Soulmates, each logged interaction has its own chance (changeFriendRate) to make you Illusory Friends: 3% for multiplayer dungeon clears and PvP battles, 2% for team Alpha/Omega kills, Aniimo exchanges and gifting an Aniimo in Companion Mode, 1% for chatting, home visits and emotes. When it hits you'll see "You and {friend} are now Illusory Friends!"

  3. Only one Illusory Friend at a time

    You have a single Illusory Friend slot (specialFriendUId). Until then, Illusory options show "Not Illusory Friends yet". Blocking a friend ends the friendship and resets Affinity.

  4. Do the Aniimo Illusory together

    Once you're Illusory Friends, you'll get an "Illusory Invite: You can perform Aniimo Illusory now." Use Go to Illusory from the friend's Affinity panel. Each of you picks one of your own Aniimo, both confirm, and the pair cuddle and become Illusory. Aniimo that are dispatched, placed in your Home, or in your team / exploring / in battle can't be picked ("This Aniimo cannot be selected."). If either side backs out you'll see "Illusory interrupted" / "The friend has canceled Illusory."

  5. Mind the limit: 1 Illusified Aniimo

    VARIANT_LIMIT_1 = 1. If you already have one, the game warns: "You've reached your Illusory Aniimo limit… illusifying this Aniimo will revert your other Aniimo to its original form." The Aniimo's details page shows Illusifying Friend and Illusification Date, and the Aniimo box has an Illusory filter.

What being Illusory does

  • The Aniimo gets the Illusory (VARIANT) label, a separate rarity flag next to Shiny, Elite, Rainbow and Umbral/Dark, with its own icon .
  • PetVariantUtils.genVariantInfo can roll extra potential (IV) level-ups when an Aniimo is illusified (cap CHANGE_PET_IV_MAX = 5). In the current data, the roll table at every Affinity level is changeAdd = {0: 100}, a 100% weight on 0 extra level-ups, and the re-roll chance changeRate is 0. So in this version illusifying doesn't raise stats. What changes is the Illusory label and look. The server could still apply its own rules.
  • Illusory status also counts for breeding: Utils has variantGeneticProb / variantBreedRandomProb, so offspring can inherit the trait (exact odds not in client data).
