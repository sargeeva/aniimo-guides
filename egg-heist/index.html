Eggs

Every egg you can carry out of Egg Heist. Inside a match you pick up a match copy of each egg. Submit it to the Egg Boat and evacuate successfully, and the whole team gets the real egg.

Loot pools

The item pools that the sources above draw from. Supplies, relic collectibles, keys and essences can be extracted. In PvP, gear and chips stay in the match.

Rank rewards

Evacuate successfully to earn Rank Points. Each new rank pays out once, and the first rank of each tier also gives that tier's Rank-Up Gift Box.

Daily Lucky Chests

A successful extraction earns a Lucky Chest of random quality. It unlocks after a timer, and higher ranks raise the odds of better chests. Chest contents are rolled on the server.

About the data

  • Modes and difficulty numbers come from the game's own difficulty constants: 3 Normal, 4 Hard, 5 Nightmare, 6 Chaos, plus PvP. Each container and enemy keeps a separate drop list per difficulty.
  • Main loot is the source's primary roll. Bonus loot is a second roll on top of it. When the game client only holds a pointer to a server-side package, the source shows Server-side.
  • Most chests, sarcophagi, Obsidian guards and Sanctum enemies have a client-listed bonus pool on every difficulty (supplies, essences). Their main roll, and everything from egg nests, pots and patrols, is rolled on the server.
  • Rarity colours follow the game's quality tiers: Common Uncommon Rare Epic Legendary Prismatic.
